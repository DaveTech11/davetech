FAQ

Questions before you start?

Here are the common ones. If yours isn’t here, send the project anyway and we’ll figure it out together.

Ask about a project
How long does a website take?

Simple Starter builds can be ready in 24–48 hours. Larger Standard and Premium projects usually take several days depending on pages, content, integrations and feedback.

Can I request a category that isn’t listed?

Yes. The category list is only a starting point. Tell us what your business does and what the site needs to accomplish.

Can you use my own photos and logo?

Absolutely. Send your brand assets, or ask us to use a curated visual direction. The restaurant image on this page is an example of the visual style you can request.

Can the site have lots of images?

Yes. We can build galleries, masonry grids, full-screen viewers, before/after sections, project case studies and image-led landing pages.

Can I add booking, payments or a store?

Yes. Those are common add-ons. The exact integration depends on the service you use and the workflow you want customers to follow.

Do you deploy the website?

Yes. Deployment help is included. We can work with platforms such as Render or other suitable hosting setups and guide you through domain connection.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Mobile responsiveness is part of the build, with layouts tested around small screens as well as desktop widths.

What if I don’t have website text yet?

That’s fine. Send the basic facts about your business. We can structure the page content and you can revise it before launch.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Send the current URL or screenshots and explain what you want changed. We can redesign the visual system or rebuild the experience from the ground up.

How do I start?

Use the project form below. Include your business, category, desired package and what you want the website to do. We’ll use that to scope the next step.
